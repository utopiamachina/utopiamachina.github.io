About this policy

This policy explains how Utopia Machina handles personal information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and your consent to receive news and updates.

How we use it

This information is only used to send the updates you requested, manage your subscription, and understand basic newsletter performance.

Newsletter provider

We use Brevo to process and store newsletter subscriptions. Subscriptions use double opt-in, so you must confirm your email address before receiving updates.

Your choices

Every newsletter will include a link to unsubscribe or update your preferences. You can also contact us to ask what information we hold or request its deletion.

Cookies and analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website—for example, which pages are visited, general session activity, approximate location, and browser or device type. This helps us improve the site and understand interest in our games.

Google Analytics is completely disabled until you select “Accept analytics.” If you reject non-essential cookies, its script is not loaded and no analytics events are sent. If accepted, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga. Advertising storage, Google Signals, and ad-personalisation features are disabled.

Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage. You can change or withdraw consent at any time through the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics collection and removes Google Analytics cookies accessible to this site. You can read more in Google’s Privacy Policy.
