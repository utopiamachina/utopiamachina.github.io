About this policy

This policy explains how Utopia Machina handles personal information submitted through this website.

Information we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect your email address and the update categories you select. The preview form is not connected to a mailing service yet, so it does not currently submit personal information.

How we use it

Once signup is enabled, this information will only be used to send the updates you requested, manage your subscription, and understand basic newsletter performance.

Newsletter provider

We plan to use Brevo to process and store newsletter subscriptions. This section will be checked against the final setup before subscriptions are enabled.

Your choices

Every newsletter will include a link to unsubscribe or update your preferences. You can also contact us to ask what information we hold or request its deletion.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the site does not use analytics or set tracking cookies. If that changes, this policy will be updated before those tools are enabled.
